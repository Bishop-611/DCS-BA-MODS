# Contributing

Community contributions are welcome through pull requests.

## Workflow

1. Fork this repository and create a branch for your change.
2. Keep changes focused on one project or issue where practical.
3. Use English for documentation, comments, commit messages, issues,
   and pull request titles and descriptions.
4. Explain what changed, why it is needed, and how you verified it.
   For DCS changes, include the relevant DCS version and test conditions.
5. Open a pull request targeting `main` and respond to review feedback.

The repository owner, Bishop-611, reviews contributions and makes the
final decision on whether to merge them. A pull request does not grant
direct write access. Community contributions should not be pushed
directly to `main`.

## License and ownership

Contributions are accepted under the [DCS-BA-MODS Non-Commercial License](LICENSE).
You retain copyright in your contribution. Submit only material you own or
have permission to contribute under compatible terms.

Identify third-party materials and include their required notices and
licenses. Do not submit proprietary DCS or other third-party files without
permission to redistribute them.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue describing the affected project, steps to reproduce,
expected and actual behavior, and relevant logs or screenshots.
Remove credentials and personal information before uploading files.
