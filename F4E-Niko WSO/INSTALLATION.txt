# F4E-Niko WSO v10

The rear WSO now uses the approved MiG-29-derived ejection poses. Niko follows the parachute descent, landing, recovery and walking sequence with her own shoulder spacing and arm lengths. The skirt lies across the thighs in the occupied seat and covers the hips through the airborne and ground poses.

The update replaces the four dedicated occupied rear-seat models and the four separate rear WSO models. After seat separation, the seat occupant follows the original visibility switch. All four detail levels carry the fitted skirt. Both ground movement inputs use the approved walking cycle.

The full installer includes the rear cockpit and exterior character, first-person body, Jester portrait and voice, Tape Player tracks and FOX PLATOON livery.

## Install or restore

Close DCS and run **F4E-Niko-WSO-v10.exe**. Select the DCS installation and active Saved Games profile. Choose **Install All** to install or upgrade, or **Restore All** to restore the original files. Keep the **NikoWSO_Backups** folder from the first installation.

## Validation

The eight exported models were checked against the reviewed Blender animation. The maximum skirt control-point deviation over the sampled animation was 0.26 mm. Character visibility and retained seat and parachute geometry were checked at each detail level. Confirmation of the ejection sequence inside a live F-4E mission is pending.
