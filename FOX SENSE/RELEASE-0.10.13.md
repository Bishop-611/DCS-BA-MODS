# FOX SENSE 0.10.13

Fixes stretched HUD text, target/RWR/missile symbols, flight instruments and the SA map on non-16:9 displays. Proportions update automatically with the DCS window; projected target centers are preserved. Confirmed by the user at 3440x1440.

Close DCS and FOX SENSE before updating. Use the Setup EXE, EXE ZIP or Manual ZIP and its matching checksum. Keep settings and restoration backups; preserve other mods' shared scripts.

Offline display and installer checks passed. VR, custom multi-camera layouts, multiplayer IC and unrelated head-motion/FOV alignment are not newly validated.
